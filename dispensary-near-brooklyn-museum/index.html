Culture-District Adjacent

A licensed dispensary at the edge of Eastern Parkway.

The Brooklyn Museum, the Brooklyn Botanic Garden, and the Central Library at Grand Army Plaza form one of New York City's great cultural clusters, gathered along the northern edge of Prospect Park where Eastern Parkway meets Washington Avenue. Take N' Toke sits just up the hill at 994 Atlantic Avenue — roughly a 10-minute walk straight north of the museum.

That makes us a natural stop for anyone exploring the district. A day at the museum, a stroll through the cherry blossoms, or a First Saturdays evening can easily fold in a visit to a licensed, walk-in dispensary on the way home. Everything we carry is lab-tested and sold by knowledgeable budtenders from New York-licensed cultivators.

We are open daily until 10:00 PM, walk-in only — no appointment, no online ordering, no delivery. Just bring a valid government photo ID showing you are 21 or older. Remember that cannabis cannot be consumed at the museum, in the garden, or in any public space — anything you pick up is for later, at home.

The Cultural Cluster

Three landmarks, one short walk.

Eastern Parkway gathers some of Brooklyn's most-visited cultural institutions within a few minutes of one another — and of our door.

01 / 03

Brooklyn Museum

New York's third-largest museum, home to an encyclopedic collection and the celebrated First Saturdays program — free admission and live programming on the first Saturday of most months, roughly 5–11 PM.

02 / 03

Brooklyn Botanic Garden

Fifty-two acres of cultivated gardens next door to the museum, famous for its cherry blossom esplanade, Japanese Hill-and-Pond Garden, and seasonal conservatories.

03 / 03

Central Library at Grand Army Plaza

The Brooklyn Public Library's landmark Art Deco flagship anchors the northern edge of Prospect Park, completing the Eastern Parkway cultural triangle.

Walk From the Museum

Door to door in four steps.

A flat, leafy walk along Washington Avenue connects the museum cluster to our shop — no transfers, no detours.

  1. 01

    Start at 994 Atlantic Avenue

    From Take N' Toke's door on Atlantic Avenue, head to the corner of Atlantic and Washington Avenue — Washington runs straight south toward the museum.

  2. 02

    Walk south on Washington Avenue

    Follow Washington Avenue south through the heart of Prospect Heights. You will pass the brownstone blocks and cafés that define the neighborhood — a flat, leafy walk.

  3. 03

    Cross Eastern Parkway

    After about eight blocks you reach Eastern Parkway, the tree-lined boulevard designed by Olmsted and Vaux. The Brooklyn Museum's grand facade sits just across it.

  4. 04

    Arrive at the cultural cluster

    You are now at the Brooklyn Museum, with the Brooklyn Botanic Garden and the Central Library at Grand Army Plaza all within a few minutes. Total walk from our shop: about 10 minutes.

FAQ

Brooklyn Museum questions.

05 questions · answered

All FAQ
Is there a cannabis dispensary near the Brooklyn Museum?

Yes. Take N' Toke is a licensed adult-use dispensary at 994 Atlantic Avenue — about a 10-minute walk north of the Brooklyn Museum, roughly eight blocks up Washington Avenue. It is one of the closest licensed, walk-in dispensaries to the Eastern Parkway cultural district.

How far is Take N' Toke from the Brooklyn Museum?

About a 10-minute walk. From our door at 994 Atlantic Avenue, head south on Washington Avenue and cross Eastern Parkway — the museum is right there. By train, take the 2 or 3 to Eastern Parkway–Brooklyn Museum.

Can I stop in around a First Saturdays visit?

Yes. The Brooklyn Museum's First Saturdays run roughly 5–11 PM, and we are open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, so you can browse before heading over. Bring a valid government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. Please note that cannabis cannot be consumed at the museum or in public spaces.

Is Take N' Toke near the Brooklyn Botanic Garden too?

Yes. The Brooklyn Botanic Garden sits right beside the Brooklyn Museum, so it is the same short walk — about 10 minutes from our shop, or a quick ride to the Eastern Parkway–Brooklyn Museum or Prospect Park stations.

Do I need to order ahead or can I just walk in?

Take N' Toke is walk-in only — there is no online ordering, reservations, or delivery. You can browse our live menu online to plan your visit, then shop in person with help from our budtenders.
