Ch. 02

The Legal Route

The Legal Ways to Obtain Adult-Use Cannabis in Brooklyn

In New York, adult-use cannabis became legal for adults 21 and over under the Marihuana Regulation and Taxation Act (MRTA), and retail sales are overseen by the New York Office of Cannabis Management (OCM). For the everyday Brooklyn consumer, that means one straightforward path: buy from a licensed adult-use dispensary.

A licensed dispensary holds an active OCM retail license, sells only lab-tested products sourced from New York-licensed cultivators, checks every customer's ID for 21+, and uses compliant, child-resistant packaging. Brooklyn now has a growing number of these legal storefronts, including Take N' Toke at 994 Atlantic Avenue on the Prospect Heights / Clinton Hill border.

A quick note on terms: “adult-use” (sometimes called recreational) is for anyone 21+. New York also has a medical cannabis program with its own certified-patient process. This guide covers the adult-use path, which is what most people mean when they search for how to get cannabis in Brooklyn. No medical card is needed to shop at an adult-use dispensary — just a valid government-issued ID showing you are 21 or older.

Route Diverged

Walk-In vs. “Delivery”: What Those Brooklyn Search Results Actually Mean

If you search “weed delivery Brooklyn” or “cannabis delivery Prospect Heights,” you'll see a lot of results — and it's worth understanding what you're looking at. Many storefronts that advertise delivery online are not licensed by the OCM, and buying cannabis from an unlicensed source means the product hasn't gone through New York's required lab testing and oversight.

Licensed retail in New York is built around the in-store experience. The clearest, simplest way for a Brooklyn consumer to know they're buying legal, lab-tested product is to walk into a licensed dispensary and buy in person. That's the model Take N' Toke uses: browse the live menu online, shop in person. You see exactly what's available, you talk to staff, and you know the product is compliant.

So when you weigh “how do I get cannabis in Brooklyn,” the dependable answer isn't the flashiest delivery ad — it's verifying a store's OCM license and visiting a licensed dispensary.

Ch. 06

Verify

How to Verify a Brooklyn Dispensary's License in Under a Minute

New York makes this easy. You don't have to take a shop's word for it — check the state's own records.

  1. 1
    Open the New York Office of Cannabis Management Dispensary Location Verification tool at cannabis.ny.gov/dispensary-location-verification.
  2. 2
    Search by the shop's name or street address.
  3. 3
    Confirm the dispensary appears with an active license at that exact address.
  4. 4
    In the store, scan the official OCM verification QR sticker in the window and look for the posted license. A legal shop displays both.

If a shop is not in the OCM tool, has no posted license, and has no official QR sticker, it is not a licensed dispensary.

Why Buying From a Licensed Brooklyn Store Matters

Buying from a licensed dispensary isn't just about following the rules — it's about knowing what you're actually getting. When you shop legal, the regulated supply chain backs every purchase.

  • Packaging carries accurate labels and the required New York warnings.
  • Product is sourced from a regulated New York supply chain.
  • There is a regulated channel to address anything wrong with a product.
  • Staff check ID for 21+, keeping adult-use product away from minors.
  • You get a known product, a real receipt, and budtenders who can answer your questions.
