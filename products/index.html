Quality Assurance

Every product is tested.

New York State requires OCM-licensed dispensaries to sell only lab-tested cannabis. We go further by verifying test results, maintaining proper storage conditions, and training our staff to explain exactly what is in each product.

  1. 01

    Lab Tested

    Every product on our shelves is tested by an OCM-approved laboratory for potency, pesticides, heavy metals, mold, and residual solvents.

  2. 02

    Tracked Inventory

    We use New York's seed-to-sale tracking system. Every product has a verifiable chain of custody from cultivation to your hands.

  3. 03

    Proper Storage

    Temperature and humidity-controlled storage for flower and concentrates. Strict FIFO rotation so nothing sits on shelves past its prime.

  4. 04

    Clear Labeling

    All products display THC/CBD content, terpene profiles where available, batch numbers, and test dates. No guesswork.

If a product does not meet our standards, it does not make it to the shelf. We would rather carry fewer products and trust every one of them.

Guidance

How to choose a product.

Common questions we get at the counter. If you still aren't sure, just ask us.

?

How do I choose between product types?

Start with your goals. Want fast onset? Try flower or vapes. Want long-lasting relief? Edibles or tinctures. Want convenience? Pre-rolls. Our staff can narrow it down based on your preferences.

?

What if I have never used cannabis before?

Start with a low-THC flower, a 5mg edible, or a tincture where you control the dose drop by drop. Avoid concentrates until you understand your tolerance. Our staff specializes in first-time guidance.

?

How do I read a product label?

Look for the THC percentage (flower) or milligrams (edibles/tinctures), the strain name, and the terpene profile. Higher THC does not always mean better. We can walk you through any label in the store.

?

Do you have CBD-only products?

Yes. We carry products across the THC/CBD spectrum, from CBD-dominant to balanced ratios to high-THC. Tell us what effect you want and we will point you to the right ratio.
