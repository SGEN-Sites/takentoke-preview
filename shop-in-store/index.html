The Process

Four steps to your first purchase.

The whole thing takes about 10 to 15 minutes. Here is how it works.

  1. 01
    Stage 01 of 04

    Check in at the door

    A staff member will greet you and check your government-issued photo ID. This is required by New York law for every customer, every visit. Once verified, you are welcome to come in and look around.

  2. 02
    Stage 02 of 04

    Browse or ask for help

    You can browse our display cases on your own, or ask a budtender for recommendations. If you tell us your experience level and what you are looking for, we can narrow down the options. There is no pressure to decide quickly.

  3. 03
    Stage 03 of 04

    Choose your products

    Once you have found what you want, let a budtender know. They will pull the product, explain the label (potency, dosage, suggested use), and answer any remaining questions. You can change your mind at any point.

  4. 04
    Stage 04 of 04

    Pay and go

    Complete your purchase at the register. We accept cash and debit cards. An ATM is available on-site. Your products are placed in a sealed, opaque exit bag as required by New York law. The entire process typically takes 10 to 15 minutes.

  5. Liftoff

Before You Come

What to bring.

Bring 01

Valid photo ID (21+)

Accepted forms include a state driver's license, state ID card, US passport, US military ID, or a valid passport from another country. The ID must not be expired.

Bring 02

Cash or debit card

We accept cash and debit cards. We do not accept credit cards. There is an ATM on-site for your convenience.

Checklist

  • Valid photo ID (21+)
  • Cash or debit card
  • Questions (write them down if it helps)
  • An open mind

That's it. You do not need a medical card, a reservation, or any prior cannabis knowledge. We handle the rest.

Visiting the Dispensary

All FAQs
Do you offer delivery?

No. Take N' Toke is a walk-in retail dispensary only. We do not offer delivery or online ordering. New York State adult-use cannabis regulations require in-person purchases with valid ID verification. We are happy to assist you when you visit.

What are your hours?

We are open 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM, 7 days a week. Last call is 15 minutes before close. Holiday hours may vary and are posted in advance on our homepage.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cannabis is a cash-heavy industry due to federal banking regulations. We accept cash and debit cards. We do not accept credit cards. There is an ATM on-site for your convenience. Exact payment methods may change as banking regulations evolve.

What ID do I need to bring?

You must bring a valid, government-issued photo ID proving you are 21 years of age or older. Accepted forms include a state driver's license, state ID card, US passport, US military ID, or a valid passport from another country. The ID must not be expired. We check every customer, every time — no exceptions.

What's the experience like for a first-time customer?

We are set up to welcome first-time customers. When you walk in, a staff member will check your ID. Then you can browse or speak with a budtender who will ask about your experience level, what effects you're looking for, and any preferences or concerns. There is no pressure to buy anything specific, and we are happy to explain everything. The whole process usually takes 10 to 15 minutes.
