12 Neighborhoods

Walking directions from your neighborhood.

We serve 12 Brooklyn neighborhoods from our Atlantic Avenue location. Here is how to get here from each one.

2-5 min0.3 mi

Prospect Heights

Walk east or west along Atlantic Avenue. We are between Washington and Classon Avenues. Most of Prospect Heights is within a few blocks.

View Neighborhood Page 8-15 min0.8 mi

Crown Heights

Head west along Atlantic Avenue or take Washington Avenue south to Atlantic. From Franklin Avenue, walk west on Atlantic for about 10 minutes.

View Neighborhood Page 5-10 min0.9 mi

Clinton Hill

Walk south on Washington, Vanderbilt, or Clinton Avenue to Atlantic Avenue, then head east. Most of Clinton Hill is within a 10-minute walk.

View Neighborhood Page 8-12 min0.7 mi

Fort Greene

Head south through Fort Greene Park to Atlantic Avenue, or walk east along Fulton Street and turn south. The Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center station connects easily.

View Neighborhood Page 10-15 min0.6 mi

Boerum Hill

Walk east along Atlantic Avenue from the Boerum Hill area. Pass the Barclays Center and continue to 994 Atlantic between Washington and Classon.

View Neighborhood Page 12-18 min1.1 mi

Park Slope

Walk north on any avenue toward Atlantic Avenue, then head east. From the northern end of Park Slope near Flatbush Avenue, it is about 12 minutes.

View Neighborhood Page 12-20 min1.2 mi

Bed-Stuy

Walk south on any north-south avenue to Atlantic Avenue, then head west. Alternatively, take the C train one stop to Clinton-Washington and walk 2 blocks.

View Neighborhood Page 10-15 min0.5 mi

Downtown Brooklyn

Walk east along Atlantic Avenue past the Barclays Center. From Flatbush Avenue, head east on Atlantic for about 10 minutes.

View Neighborhood Page 15-20 min1.3 mi

Brooklyn Heights

Take the 2/3 train from Clark Street or Borough Hall to Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center, then walk 1 block. Walking takes about 20 minutes via Atlantic Avenue.

View Neighborhood Page 12-18 min1 mi

Cobble Hill

Walk east along Atlantic Avenue from the Cobble Hill area. The walk follows Atlantic Avenue the entire way and passes through Boerum Hill.

View Neighborhood Page 15-25 min2.1 mi

Flatbush

Take the B/Q train from Prospect Park or Church Avenue to Seventh Avenue station, then walk 3 blocks east. Or take the 2/5 to Atlantic Ave-Barclays Center.

View Neighborhood Page 20-30 min2.8 mi

Williamsburg

Take the G train to Fulton Street, then transfer to the C to Clinton-Washington (2 blocks away). Or take the J/M to Myrtle Avenue and walk south.
